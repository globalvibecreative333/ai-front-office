§ I. Who This Is For You may already recognize this
  • You're considering a sale, succession, retirement, or leadership transition within the next several years.
  • The business still relies on you to make important decisions or handle the unusual situations that don't fit a manual.
  • Your employees know their part of the work, but no one — including you — has the complete picture written down.
  • Dormant customers, old leads, missed inquiries, weak referrals, or inconsistent follow-up represent real, recoverable opportunity you haven't gotten to.
  • You want the company to stay valuable and functional beyond your personal presence — whether or not you leave anytime soon.
§ II. Why This Matters Financially Operations and value are the same problem

A buyer or successor needs more than financial statements. They need to understand how the business actually works.

Operations and value are the same problem.

Owner dependence makes a business harder to run without the owner. Undocumented knowledge creates transition risk. Revenue leakage means the business may be leaving money behind before a sale or transition.

Poorly organized operations make due diligence and handoff more difficult.

§ IV. The Knowledge-Extraction Experience Not a long interview

You don't sit through a conventional, long-form interview with April. ChatGPT or Claude asks one question at a time, and follows the threads that matter based on what you say. You respond however is easiest — by voice or by text — and sessions pause and resume, so you don't have to get it all out in one sitting.

The system is specifically listening for decisions, exceptions, relationships, judgment calls, red flags, and escalation logic — the things that don't show up in a job description. You review and validate everything it produces before it becomes a final asset.

April designs the extraction system, governs what it produces, and assembles the final materials. The AI reduces how much time and energy the interview costs you — it doesn't run the engagement, and it doesn't replace your participation.

§ VII. Who This Is — And Isn't — For Fit matters more than enthusiasm

Good fit

  • Established, owner-operated businesses
  • Existing customers, leads, or meaningful relationships to work with
  • A real transition horizon — succession, retirement, sale, or leadership change
  • Important knowledge still concentrated in the owner
  • Willingness to provide source material and validate the work

Not the right fit

  • Empty startups without operating history
  • Owners looking only for a valuation number
  • Businesses seeking legal, accounting, brokerage, or financial advice
  • Owners unwilling to participate in knowledge validation
  • Anyone expecting guaranteed revenue, valuation, or sale results
