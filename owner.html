The knowledge-extraction experience

Not a long interview. Not a form to fill out alone.

This is the part that's genuinely different from "write down your SOPs" — and it's worth understanding before you commit.

  • You don't sit through a conventional, long-form interview with April. ChatGPT or Claude asks one question at a time, and follows the threads that matter based on what you say.
  • You respond however is easiest — by voice or by text.
  • Sessions pause and resume. You don't have to get it all out in one sitting.
  • The system is specifically listening for decisions, exceptions, relationships, judgment calls, red flags, and escalation logic — the things that don't show up in a job description.
  • You review and validate everything the process produces before it becomes a final asset. Nothing goes into your handoff materials without your sign-off.

April's role in this

April designs the extraction system, governs what it produces, and assembles the final materials. The AI reduces how much time and energy the interview costs you — it doesn't run the engagement, and it doesn't replace your participation.

You still have to show up and answer. That part can't be automated. What changes is how much easier it is to do.

The AI lowers the burden of the interview. It does not eliminate your role in it.
